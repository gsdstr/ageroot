# Summary-first confirmation

**Status:** accepted

Ageroot dry-run confirmation is based on a concise summary rather than a mandatory pre-confirmation full diff. The summary reports counts for changed and unchanged paths, and path-level reason, apply eligibility, and full-diff access for conflicts and blocked paths; complete diffs remain available on request. This accepts a review-depth trade-off in favour of a faster confirmation flow while retaining explicit approval and atomic apply.
